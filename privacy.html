📋१. ॲपमध्ये कोणती माहिती भरली जाते?

शालेय पोषण आहाराचे दैनंदिन कामकाज व शासकीय प्रपत्रे तयार करण्यासाठी वापरकर्ते (मुख्याध्यापक / शिक्षक) खालील माहिती स्वतः ॲपमध्ये भरतात:

  • शाळेची माहिती: शाळेचे नाव, केंद्र, तालुका, जिल्हा, UDISE क्रमांक आणि इयत्ता.
  • दैनंदिन व मासिक नोंदी: इयत्तानिहाय विद्यार्थी पटसंख्या, उपस्थिती, लाभार्थी ताटे, दैनंदिन पाककृती मेनू आणि पूरक आहार.
  • धान्य साठा व मागणी: तांदूळ व इतर धान्यादी मालाचा सुरुवातीचा साठा, आवक-जावक नोंदी आणि मासिक धान्य मागणी.
💡 महत्त्वाचे: या ॲपमध्ये विद्यार्थ्यांची वैयक्तिक नावे, आधार क्रमांक, बँक खाते किंवा कोणतीही संवेदनशील वैयक्तिक माहिती मागितली जात नाही.

Privacy Policy

"MDM Poshan Aahar Register" is a free Progressive Web Application (PWA) designed for schools to manage daily PM POSHAN mid-day meal attendance, foodgrain stock, and monthly official reports.

🛡️ No Third-Party Server 🚫 Zero Ads ☁️ User-Owned Google Drive Sync ⚡ Works Offline

📋1. Information Stored in the App

To generate daily calculations and official monthly school reports, teachers/headmasters voluntarily enter the following school-level data:

  • School Profile: School name, cluster (Kendra), block (Taluka), district, UDISE code, and active classes.
  • Daily & Monthly Entries: Class-wise enrollment (roll), attendance, beneficiary plates served, daily recipe menu, and supplementary nutrition records.
  • Grain Stock & Demand: Opening balance, stock-in/stock-out transactions, and monthly foodgrain demand quantities.
💡 Note: The app does NOT collect or require students' personal names, Aadhaar numbers, bank details, or any sensitive personal information.

💾2. Where & How Is Your Data Stored?

  • Local Device Storage: All data entered by you is stored locally inside your own phone or computer browser's localStorage.
  • Google Drive Backup (Optional): When you enable and click "Google Drive Sync", your app data is saved directly into your own personal Google Drive inside a dedicated folder named MDM_Data as JSON files.
  • Offline Support: Using Service Worker caching, the app works seamlessly offline without an active internet connection.

🔐3. Google Drive Permissions & Limited Use Compliance

For cloud backup and synchronization, the app uses official Google Identity Services and the Google Drive API:

  • The app requests only the restricted, non-invasive scope: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file.
  • This means the app can ONLY view, create, and update the specific files and folder (MDM_Data) created by this app itself.
  • The app CANNOT see, read, modify, or access any other personal files, photos, or documents in your Google Drive.
  • Google API Services User Data Policy: Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs strictly adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

🚫4. What We Do NOT Do (Zero Server Storage)

  • We do NOT transmit or store your school data on any external or developer-owned server.
  • The developer has zero access to your local data or your Google Drive files.
  • We do NOT sell, rent, trade, or share user data with any third parties, and the app contains zero advertisements.
  • Third-party services used are limited to Google Fonts (for Devanagari typography), Google Sign-In (for optional Drive authentication), and basic Google Analytics (for aggregate page-view statistics).

🗑️5. Data Retention, Deletion & Revoking Access

You retain 100% ownership and control over your data at all times:

  • Delete Local Data: Clearing your browser's site data/storage immediately deletes all records stored on your device.
  • Delete Google Drive Backup: You can delete the MDM_Data folder directly from your Google Drive at any time.
  • Revoke App Permissions: You can revoke this app's access to your Google account at any time by visiting your Google Account Security page (myaccount.google.com/permissions).

⚖️6. Terms of Use

By accessing and using this application, you agree to the following terms and conditions:

  • Free Educational Utility: This web application is provided "as-is" and free of charge to assist teachers and headmasters in simplifying daily PM POSHAN mid-day meal calculations and record-keeping.
